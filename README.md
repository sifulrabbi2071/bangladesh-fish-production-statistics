# Bangladesh Fish Production Statistics, 1983-84 to 2024-25: Summary Tables and Harmonized Data

 [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23466909.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23466909)

National, waterbody-wise, species-wise and district-wise fish production of Bangladesh, compiled from **23 Department of Fisheries yearbooks** (2002-03 to 2024-25), with the national series back to **1983-84**.

**Compiled by:** Md Siful Islam Rabbi, Noakhali Science and Technology University, Noakhali-3814, Bangladesh

**Version:** 1.0 (October 2026) · **License:** [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE)

> Not an official publication of the Department of Fisheries (DoF), Bangladesh. All figures come from DoF yearbooks.

## What is inside

| Folder | For whom | Files |
|---|---|---|
| [`volume1/`](volume1) | Anyone who wants to **read** the numbers | Summary tables as [PDF](volume1/Bangladesh_Fish_Production_Statistics_Vol1_v1.0.pdf), Word and Excel |
| [`volume2/`](volume2) | Anyone who wants to **analyse** the data | CSV files, one Excel workbook, [README](volume2/README.md) and codebook |

**Volume 1: Summary Tables** (14 pages)
1. Total production: inland capture, inland culture and marine, 1983-84 to 2024-25
2. Production by waterbody, 1983-84 to 2024-25
3. Water area and production per hectare
4. Production by species group, 2002-03 to 2024-25
5. Inland production by district (64 districts), 2002-03 to 2024-25
6. Inland production by district and waterbody, 2024-25

**Volume 2: Detailed Data Files.** Every figure with its source yearbook and table, both revised and originally published values, district x waterbody and species x waterbody for every year, beel and Kaptai Lake species detail, and 56 data notes. See [`volume2/README.md`](volume2/README.md).

Unit: metric tonnes. Years are Bangladesh fiscal years (July-June), e.g. 2024-25 = July 2024 to June 2025.
